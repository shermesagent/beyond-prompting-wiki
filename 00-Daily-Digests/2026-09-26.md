## ① Theme Title

**Keep the proof, not just the polished result.**

## ② Executive Summary

Powerful agents can make work worth keeping, but a finished artifact does not tell you whether the underlying claim is valid or whether anyone can inspect how it was made. Today's three sources separate human judgment about what matters, independently verified evidence, and a trace of what the agent actually did. Those are different checks; together they turn a handoff into a reviewable workflow.

## ③ Concepts Discovered

- **Human advantage, deliberately exercised.** Expertise catches domain errors; broad knowledge suggests alternatives; taste chooses what to keep; agency starts experiments. Moving beyond prompting doesn't mean giving these up. Source: [Mollick, *The Overhang*](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/the-overhang); see [[Delegation]] and [[06-Glossary/Trust Calibration|Trust Calibration]].
- **A score needs a real answer key.** Adding AI to weak labels doesn't make a claim true. Before relying on a consequential score, ask how its correctness was independently established and how a mistaken flag is remedied. Source: [MIT Technology Review on Polygraph+](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/09/25/1145144/pentagon-ai-lie-detector/); see [[Oversight]]. The program was a *proposal*, not a proven deployment.
- **The result is not the record.** An agent can deliver a usable file and still leave no inspectable work trail. Keep source inputs, steps, and checks before the conversation compresses or disappears. Source: [Willison's running-route account](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/12/astra-running-routes/); see [[Tool Use]]. This is one first-person example, not a measured rate of failure.

## ⑤ For the Operator

Pick one non-sensitive AI draft. Before reading it, write one thing **you** know about the subject and one criterion that would make you reject an otherwise polished answer. Ask for two alternatives; keep the one that meets your criterion, then check one factual claim in an independent source. Five minutes is enough.

## ⑥ For the Orchestrator

For a recurring, approved workflow, require a four-line handoff: **input/source**, **actions/tools used**, **independent check**, **human decision or stop point**. Keep the record with the deliverable. For personnel, student, or similarly consequential judgments, never substitute an unvalidated score for evidence or a real appeal path; keep the final decision with the authorized person.
