## ① Theme Title

**The Handoff Is the Work: Translate the Request, Name the Decision, Keep the Proof**

## ② Executive Summary

A stronger agent does not fix a handoff nobody can understand or verify. Today's sources point to three moves ordinary teams can make: translate a person's work language into a checkable source, name who acts and who decides, and state what evidence would count before an AI review begins.

## ③ Concepts Discovered

- **Four questions before the handoff.** [[What Is Beyond Prompting]] draws a plain-language version from the [9A human–AI collaboration framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22948): What is the task? Who does it? Who decides? What proof counts? This is a proposed design framework, not a measured productivity gain.
- **A translation key beats a better guess.** In an [enterprise data-assistant study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23304), business users used their own terms instead of database field names. In the authors' simulation, a structured map between those terms and data structure outperformed a simpler matching method on reported measures. [[The Blank Box Problem]] already names the interface problem; [[What Is Beyond Prompting]] now offers a small public-data translation exercise. The paper does not demonstrate error-free production answers.
- **An issue closed is not every claim verified.** An [author-in-the-loop conference audit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22465) used author checklists, agent checks, author repair, and reviewer decisions. Twenty issues were raised after review across 36 papers and 133 tables and figures; follow-up supported closing them, though full reproduction remained unverified in some cases. [[Why This Matters]] translates the distinction into a low-stakes check of one public claim.

*Evidence boundary:* These are arXiv version-1 abstracts and publisher metadata, not full-text appraisals. The four-question and translation exercises are curator adaptations, not evaluated interventions. Do not upload student, staff, health, financial or other restricted records to a public AI tool.

## ⑤ For the Operator

Pick a public, low-stakes document you already know. Write the request in your normal words, then add a one-line translation key: “By **X**, I mean **this publicly available source/field**.” Ask AI for a draft only; open the cited source and check one claim yourself. If you cannot name the source, the answer stays unverified. See [[The Evidence Ticket]].

## ⑥ For the Orchestrator

For one recurring *public-data-only* task, make a four-line handoff card: **task → AI step → human decision owner → evidence for approval**. Add a declared limit (“this checks the cited figure, not every figure in the report”). Have the reviewer approve or return it for repair; keep “issue resolved” separate from “whole report verified.” This is a tabletop design pattern, not a claim of automatic compliance. See [[The Review-First Pattern]] and [[The Orchestrator Mindset]].

## ⑧ Top Insight of the Day

> **The best handoff does not make the person speak machine. It gives the machine a map of the person's work—and gives the person the last word and the evidence to use it.**
