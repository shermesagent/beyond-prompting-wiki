## ① Theme Title

**A good draft is only one part of the job**

## ② Executive Summary

An AI can produce a polished answer while hiding the route it chose, the human contribution, or the handoff that makes the answer usable. Today's research points to a practical shift: judge a workflow by the decisions people can see and own, not just by whether its final draft looks finished.

## ③ Concepts Discovered

- **The organizational test.** A technically hard task can still be the easy part of work involving owners, deadlines and competing needs. Ask who supplies missing context, who checks the evidence and who can stop the release. [Barres and colleagues](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03494v1) → [[The School District Shift]] and [[From Prompt to Pipeline]].
- **The road not taken.** An AI analysis contains choices you may never see. Displaying an alternative path reduced overreliance in one 48-person data-analysis study; ask to see one meaningful alternative before accepting the conclusion. [Grunde-McLaughlin and colleagues](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04183v1) → [[From Prompt to Pipeline]].
- **The person's part.** A polished AI-assisted product does not reveal what the learner decided. A first-year university prompt-journal study suggests looking at the person's contribution and the phase of work, not labeling a student by their prompt style. [Shakiba and Chaffey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04783v1) → [[The School District Shift]].

*Privacy and evidence boundary:* Exercises are curator translations, not evaluated interventions. Use public material or approved staff-training examples. Never paste student, accommodation, personnel, confidential customer, or credential data into an unapproved AI service. Keep publication and instructional decisions with an authorized person.

## ⑤ For the Operator

Pick one public document you were going to ask AI to summarize. Before opening a tool, write **one decision you still own** and **one original source you will check**. Review the draft against those two lines; an impressive answer is not yet a handoff.

## ⑥ For the Orchestrator

On a draft-only workflow, add four visible fields: `chosen path / plausible alternative / approval owner / stop if source or local rule is missing`. Show them beside the output, not in a buried transcript. Have the owner open one original source and approve or reject the draft before anyone sends, publishes, or uses it for a consequential decision.
