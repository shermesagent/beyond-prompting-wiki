## ① Theme Title

**Metacognitive Demand.** AI should carry more of the load without quietly taking over the decisions that build judgment.

## ② Executive Summary

Today's sources converge on the same practical warning: support can become substitution if it removes the user's decision point. Good scaffolding keeps the learner planning, explanations mark what they do not cover, memory has boundaries, stopping has authority, and oversight starts before an agent acts.

## ③ Concepts Discovered

- **[[Metacognitive Demand]]** (new concept) — the human decision that must stay alive inside an AI workflow: what to choose, monitor, explain, stop, scope, or challenge before the system carries more of the work.
- **Preserved decision demand** — AI can reduce cognitive load without deleting the learner's responsibility to plan and monitor.
- **Explanation scope** — a clear explanation can still be incomplete; users need a way to see what the explanation does not cover.
- **Memory boundaries** — persistent memory helps when it reduces repeated disclosure, but it needs scope, provenance, retention, and access controls.
- **Stop as governance** — interruptibility is not a red button; it is authority, evidence, standing, and a restart rule.

## ⑤ For the Operator

- **Do not ask only “did AI help?”** Ask: “what did I still have to decide?”
- **Keep one decision before the answer.** Before AI gives the full output, make yourself name the goal, missing information, or first move.
- **Ask the scope question.** When AI explains something, follow with: “What does this explanation not cover?”
- **Scope memory on purpose.** If you tell an AI something personal or contextual, decide where that memory is allowed to travel.

## ⑥ For the Orchestrator

- **Design support to fade.** Let AI increase help after failed decision points, but pull back once the human can proceed.
- **Write the stop rule before the run.** Stop trigger, stop authority, evidence needed, who can raise the concern, and when the workflow may restart.
- **Set the agenda first.** For any multi-step agent task, write: what good looks like, what must not happen, what requires escalation, and what evidence the agent must leave.
- **Treat memory as a permissioned asset.** Useful memory needs scope, provenance, retention, and access — otherwise convenience becomes leakage.

## ⑧ Top Insight of the Day

> **The goal is not to make AI help less. The goal is to make help arrive without stealing the decision that builds judgment.** If the human only approves, the system may be productive — but it is no longer teaching orchestration.
