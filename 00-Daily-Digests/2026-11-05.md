## ① Theme Title

**A choice is only real when someone can use it**

## ② Executive Summary

People hesitate to hand work to AI when they cannot control the first answer, inspect the evidence, or challenge the promises made on their behalf. Today's sources show three different gaps between an offered choice and a usable one: school search settings, transparency features, and the numbers used to sell adoption. The bridge is small and concrete: test the actual control, ask the affected person what they need, and measure the local result.

## ③ Concepts Discovered

- **A bundled choice is not a separate choice.** If turning off a generated search answer also turns off search, a school cannot claim it has an AI-free search setting. Verify the actual administrator controls and teach students to open original sources in the approved environment. [Dave, WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/google-is-forcing-ai-overviews-in-classrooms-schools-are-searching-for-a-way-out/) → [[Procedural Standing]] and [[The Absent Person Test]].
- **Transparency belongs to the person doing the checking.** A disclosure label alone does not supply the source, opt-out, or correction route that a user needs. Ask the user what they need to do, then test the feature in that specific task. [Smith and colleagues, arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05549v1) → [[Procedural Standing]].
- **A forecast needs its assumptions attached.** A large projected gain is a scenario, not money saved. Record sponsor, vintage, adoption assumptions, and the local outcome you will measure before using it to justify a rollout. [Gartside, WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/uk-officials-said-ai-would-add-400-billion-to-the-economy-that-figure-mainly-came-from-google/) → [[The Engagement Gap]].

## ⑤ For the Operator

Before trusting an AI summary of a public topic, open two original links. Write one sentence each: **What did the overview say? What do the sources actually support?** If the links do not support the claim, stop there rather than asking the same AI to certify itself.

## ⑥ For the Orchestrator

Use a one-page **choice card** before a draft-only pilot: `affected person / actual control available / original evidence to check / named human for correction / local measure of benefit / stop condition`. Confirm the control with an authorized administrator; ask a user whether the proposed route is usable; then run a public-material rehearsal. Keep a non-AI path available when the promised setting does not exist.
