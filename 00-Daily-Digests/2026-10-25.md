## ② Executive Summary

AI can help spot problems and suggest fixes, but the hard part is getting a verified change to the right place without losing human control. ## ③ Concepts Discovered

- **Alert is not repair.** [WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/kernel-panic-the-php-meltdown-shows-how-ai-makes-bugs-more-exploitable/) reports AI-assisted vulnerability finding *with human expertise*, millions of subsequent attempts and no successful exploitation seen by the PHP Foundation at publication. Those are separate facts, not a single story of mass compromise. An orchestrator names an owner and a tested path from warning to fix; an operator can start by asking where the official advisory lives. See [[The Control Surface]].
- **Keep the steps of a near miss separate.** [Zvi](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/the-hackening) quotes an Internet Archive operator describing a brief shutdown and human-led AI-assisted repair. This is an attributed account, not an independently audited success rate. Save `warning / observed impact / proposed fix / test / restore owner` rather than treating an AI suggestion as proof of resolution. See [[The Failure Review]].
- **Disclose the dependency, then decide who can overrule it.** In a [first-person announcement](https://stratechery.com/2026/presented-by-stripe/), Ben Thompson identifies Stripe as both sponsor and billing/access infrastructure for Stratechery, and says he retains editorial independence. The claim of independence is his, not an outside audit. For an AI-assisted publication or decision, name the provider and the person who can challenge the output or continue if that provider is unavailable. See [[The Line You Draw]].

## ⑤ For the Operator

Take five minutes with a public advisory or a public incident article. Make two columns: **reported** and **verified independently**. Place “attempt,” “confirmed impact,” and “fix tested” in separate rows; leave blank what you cannot establish. Do not run commands from the article. The win is noticing where the story ends and your own check would have to begin.

## ⑥ For the Orchestrator

For one non-production, public-data-only workflow, fill out a change card: `DEPENDENCY / OFFICIAL SOURCE / OWNER / PROPOSED CHANGE / STAGED TEST / APPROVER / RESTORE PATH / DISCLOSURE NEEDED`. Let AI draft the card but not deploy or approve anything. If a field is blank, keep the workflow draft-only and assign a human to resolve it. Save near misses with the same card so the next person inherits the lesson, not just the final output.
