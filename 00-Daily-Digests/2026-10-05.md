# Daily Digest — 2026-10-05

> **Day 1 — The Shift.** Three newly integrated sources: one October 5 essay and two preprints submitted October 1, announced in Monday’s arXiv feed.

## ① Theme Title

**A Handoff Needs a Place to Think—and a Person Who Can Take It**

## ② Executive Summary

More AI use does not necessarily mean people welcome every AI rollout. A useful move beyond prompting is to shape the task before the answer appears, then make sure a real reviewer can inspect and change what matters. Today’s sources offer an editorial interpretation and two proposed research frameworks—not measured evidence that this exact practice improves district outcomes.

## ③ Concepts Discovered

- **Use is not a yes vote:** A person may find one chatbot task helpful while resisting being pushed into an automated workflow. Before expanding AI access, ask which use helps and which use needs choice or review. [MIT Technology Review, October 5](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/10/05/1145682/people-really-hate-ai-so-why-cant-they-get-enough/). See [[The Operator Mindset]].
- **A place to think before a finished answer:** A research agenda proposes a shared workspace that turns task clarification into an interface with inspectable design rules. Before delegating, draw the fields that make a task legible to a colleague. [Conklin, Capra and North, arXiv:2610.02369](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02369). See [[What Is Beyond Prompting]] and [[The Blank Box Problem]].
- **A reviewer needs capacity, not just a name:** A proposed cyber-oversight architecture considers the human operator’s situation along with the risk of the event. For ordinary work, ask whether the reviewer has time, the evidence and a backup; this is an analogy, not a validated transfer from maritime cyber operations. [Conklin, Gao and North, arXiv:2610.02384](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02384). See [[The Orchestrator Mindset]].

## ⑤ For the Operator

Pick one low-risk task using public information. Write two lines: “AI helps me with ___” and “I would still want a choice about ___.” Then draw four fields a colleague would need to assign the task well. Keep the exercise on paper or in an approved local document.

## ⑥ For the Orchestrator

On a draft-only public notice, create a review slot: `DECISION / EVIDENCE LINK / REVIEWER / TIME AVAILABLE / BACKUP / STOP RULE`. Insert one deliberate error in your private test draft and check whether the reviewer can find and correct it before any release. If not, keep the workflow draft-only; an unacknowledged approval request is not approval.

## ⑧ Top Insight of the Day

> **A handoff is not safer because you named a reviewer. It is safer when that person has the evidence, time, and authority to change the result.**
