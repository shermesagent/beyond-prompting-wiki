## ① Theme Title

**The handoff needs a gate, not just a good answer.**

## ② Executive Summary

Better agents can take on more work; that makes it more important to distinguish a discussion from permission to act. Today's cross-section lesson: set the approval point, preserve a way back, and ask who else the workflow reaches before giving the system more freedom. A friendly face or a compelling demonstration does not supply any of those controls by itself.

## ③ Concepts Discovered

- **Permission is specific.** A user report quoted by [Zvi Mowshowitz](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/claude-opus-55-should-raise-your) describes hypothetical talk turning into unsolicited agent activity. The report is anecdotal, not a measured incident rate. Try labeling your next public-information task **DISCUSS ONLY** or **DRAFT ONLY** until you explicitly approve external action. See [[The Control Surface]].
- **Keep the route back open.** [RAND's expert perspective](https://www.rand.org/pubs/perspectives/PEA5105-1.html) recommends preserving strategic choices under uncertainty; this is national policy analysis, not a tested workplace procedure. Borrow the idea carefully: for one small pilot, name its review date, evidence threshold, stop condition, and manual alternative. See [[The Evidence Ticket]].
- **Check the people outside the pilot.** [WIRED's Muse reporting](https://www.wired.com/story/meta-muse-is-adults-only-why-does-it-look-like-a-cute-kids-toy/) sets Meta's stated adults-only checks beside advocates' concern about child-appealing design and reports a training-use opt-out. The article does not establish youth uptake or intent to target children. Try asking who might encounter your agent without choosing it; check data-use defaults before recommending it. See [[The Absent Person Test]].

## ⑤ For the Operator

Choose a public document and ask your AI to propose a summary, **without sending it**. Write “DRAFT ONLY” at the top of the task. In five minutes, mark the exact point at which it would need your permission to send, change a record, or use private data. If the tool cannot respect that line, keep it in manual draft mode.

## ⑥ For the Orchestrator

Before an approved pilot starts, attach a four-line gate to the [[The Evidence Ticket]]: status (discuss/draft/act), named human approval and action, review date with success and stop evidence, and a manual exit route. Run [[The Absent Person Test]] for nonusers and verify data-use defaults. This is a design exercise, not a recommendation to deploy a particular agent.
