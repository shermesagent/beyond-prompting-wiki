## ① Theme Title

**The answer is only one part of a good handoff**

## ② Executive Summary

Moving beyond prompting means designing the check before the work starts: what result is needed, which steps are allowed, what must not change, and whether the finished work fits its audience. A confident answer with a source link is still a claim until a person can open the evidence and judge it.

## ③ Concepts Discovered

- **The route matters as much as the result.** A [customer-management agent benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32004) tests actions, order, messages, final state and protected state—not merely the last reply. For the operator-to-orchestrator shift, write down an allowed route and a “must not change” rule before granting an AI task more freedom. See [[What Is Beyond Prompting]].
- **Fit is a real review question.** In [first-year software-engineering student journals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32118), students flagged whether plausible outputs suited their task more often than explicit correctness faults. An orchestrator makes the audience and purpose part of the brief, instead of treating “no obvious error” as approval. See [[Why This Matters]].
- **A cue is not evidence.** A [study of factuality indicators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32883) found that confidence and source cues shaped initial impressions, but those effects disappeared after people learned whether the answer was correct. The shift is from accepting the AI's presentation to checking the passage that supports the claim. See [[The Evidence Interface]].

*Evidence and privacy boundary:* These quick exercises are curator translations, **not tested interventions**. Use public or approved data only. No student or personnel records, confidential documents or live record changes should go into an unapproved tool; keep decisions and publication with a person.

## ⑤ For the Operator

Take a public event notice or public webpage. Before asking AI for a draft, jot down `RESULT / AUDIENCE / ONE CLAIM TO VERIFY`. Afterward, check the claim against its original public source and mark it confirmed or unconfirmed. If the answer is polished but fits the wrong audience, count that as a real correction, not a cosmetic edit.

## ⑥ For the Orchestrator

For a draft-only, public-data workflow, add a tiny acceptance card: `OUTCOME / ALLOWED ACTIONS / PROTECTED ITEMS / FIT FOR WHOM / EVIDENCE TO OPEN / HUMAN APPROVER`. Ask the AI to return its source passage and a short action record. Review both the final artifact and one action; hold any unsupported claim or out-of-scope action for human revision. Do not grant write access based on a benchmark score or confidence badge.
