## Executive Summary

Today's glossary deepening attacks the five foundational terms from a different angle: **precision**. Not "what do these words mean" but "how do we make them measurable?" The theme running through all five papers is *what AI leaves behind* — the residual human capability that survives (or fails to survive) delegation.

- **Understanding** → New glossary entry: **Epistemic Transfer** (Trattner, 2608.08882). The test that measures what survives when the tool is removed — ETE (Epistemic Transfer Effect) + TRC (Tool-Removal Cost) form a 4-quadrant diagnostic space: capability building, capability + tool advantage, epistemic inertness/de-skilling, verification on loan. The 30-Second Transfer Check operationalizes it.
- **Team** → Orchestration sharpened with **The Synergy Ceiling** (Kotalwar, Das & Rosé, 2606.18413). 1,482 sessions: adding AI collaborators *lowers* performance without coordination structure (shared memory, HITL gates). Team size is not a capability multiplier.
- **Trust** → Trust Calibration sharpened with **Procedure Is Not Evidence** (Williams, 2607.28869). 1,500+ legal hallucination cases: courts mandate review, but process substitution (checklists ≠ catching errors) is the trap. Calibrate on error rates, not rituals.
- **Oversight** → Oversight sharpened with **Verification Without Distrust** (Pyae, 2607.24761). Verification and trust are statistically independent — checking is routine epistemic governance, not suspicion. Evaluative vs interventionist oversight.
- **Autonomy** → Autonomy sharpened with **The Attention-Guidance Tradeoff** (Oveisi & Manjunatha, 2606.19514). Simulated SAR: LLM guidance improved efficiency but NOT total victims saved — because operators' eyes left the environment. Novices = passive reliance; experts = verification loop. Autonomy has a watching tax.

**The week's arc:** Day 1 through Day 6 has moved from the shift itself to the practice of enactment — and today the glossary becomes a set of *instruments*: each term now carries a check, a diagnostic, or a tax that makes it measurable rather than vibes-based.

## Concepts Discovered

### New Glossary Entry

- **Epistemic Transfer** (06-Glossary/Epistemic Transfer.md) — The effect of prior AI-assisted work on later *unassisted* performance on **new** problems. Four-quadrant diagnostic (ETE × TRC): capability building / capability + tool advantage / epistemic inertness / verification on loan. Includes the 30-Second Transfer Check: remove the tool, work a new instance, grade your reasoning. Distinguishes from correction effects, trust/reliance, and team performance. Complements [[Capacity Dissolution]]: that's the mechanism, this is the test. Source: Trattner, arXiv:2608.08882.
