## ① Theme Title

**The Absent Person Test — a useful AI workflow can still be someone else's problem.**

## ② Executive Summary

The person who launches an AI tool is not always the person who bears the risk. Before celebrating a faster workflow, name someone affected who did not opt in, check the benefit against something real, and give that person a usable route to a human. This is a design habit, not a demand to stop trying AI.

## ③ Concepts Discovered

- **[[The Absent Person Test]]** — name an affected nonuser *before* the pilot: what changes for them, how would we know, and what can they do about it? Why it matters: orchestration includes the people outside the tool. [Behal's smart-glasses reporting](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/09/23/1144953/smart-glasses-havoc-india/); [Dave's AT&T reporting](https://www.wired.com/story/atandt-is-automating-away-its-old-telecom-empire/).
- **A simulated person is not a consulted person.** Role-playing an audience may help you brainstorm, but it cannot give you their consent or stand in for observed outcomes. A [preprint on headline tests](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25010) found a ten-persona panel predicted real clicks worse than a plain model baseline on its reliable comparison subset. Try one real measure before you claim you know what people want.
- **[[Procedural Standing]] extends beyond users.** A person affected by recording or a changed service needs a visible human route even if they never opened the AI tool. Try writing the contact and remedy in a sentence an outsider could find.

## ⑤ For the Operator

Take one **non-sensitive** AI-assisted task and add a line to your brief: **“Someone who doesn't use this tool could be affected: ____.”** Write one potential cost and one way they could get a correction. Do not enter identifiable student, employee, or family information into an unapproved tool.

## ⑥ For the Orchestrator

Add the absent-person row to the pilot approval sheet: affected role → possible benefit and cost → *observed* measure or consented feedback → notice/alternative → named human route. Compare with today's human process; record review time and effects on people outside the team. For a district pilot, teacher authority and student privacy remain explicit gates, not boxes an AI-generated persona can check for you.
