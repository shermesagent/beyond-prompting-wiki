# From Dialogue to Task: How Cognitive Engagement With Generative Artificial Intelligence Relates to Post-Dialogue Activity and Task Outcomes

Publisher abstract read, not full paper. Preprint submitted for review, not peer reviewed. Posted 2026-11-08. Authors: Xiaoyu Zhuang, Aneesha Bakharia, Dragan Gašević, Hassan Khosravi.

The authors studied 1,681 students across 40 university courses using three AI companions for resource creation, peer moderation and practice. They coded engagement in 829 conversation threads and modeled 2,969 task sequences. Passive and active engagement predominated; deeper engagement in resource creation was associated with better peer-rated final artifacts, independently of how often students returned to the task. In practice, dialogue after an incorrect attempt was associated with fewer subsequent attempts but higher next-attempt correctness among those who continued. These are associations in higher education, not a causal test of a workplace intervention or K–12 outcomes.

Curator application (not a tested prescription): Check what the person did after the chat and whether the resulting work passed a task-specific rubric, rather than counting messages.
