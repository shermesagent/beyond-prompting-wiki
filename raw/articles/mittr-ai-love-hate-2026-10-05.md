# Source note: People really hate AI, so why can’t they get enough?

MIT Technology Review, October 5, 2026. Directly read publisher essay; byline not independently confirmed. It contrasts unfavorable public sentiment toward AI with rising chatbot use and argues, as an interpretation rather than a causal finding, that resentment may target pressure from providers rather than every useful application. The cited surveys have different populations and questions and cannot prove the same individuals both dislike and use AI. Practical test: ask a colleague separately which AI task helps them and which required rollout they oppose; do not infer support for deployment from use alone.
