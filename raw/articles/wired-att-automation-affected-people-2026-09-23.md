# Source notes — AT&T Is Automating Away Jobs—and Its Old Telecom Empire

Paresh Dave, WIRED, 2026-09-23. Read from publisher on 2026-09-24. Reported interview and company statements, not causal analysis.

AT&T describes AI for customer service, network maintenance and siting, coding and service management, while retiring copper infrastructure and cutting jobs. Its chief technology officer expects a different workforce mix including people to govern agents; he also cites efficiencies from older network retirement. These changes happen together: the reporting cannot isolate AI as the cause of layoffs or energy savings. Rural customers can be affected by landline retirement even if the company improves efficiency; regulatory approvals and local protests are reported. The rollout question must include workers and customers, not just process owners.

Try: for a proposed automation, record separately which work changes, which people may lose access or opportunity, what alternative service or role is available, and which measure would show whether the promised improvement actually occurred.
