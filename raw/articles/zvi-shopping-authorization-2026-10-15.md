# Source note: Zvi on an AI shopping proposal

Primary source: https://thezvi.substack.com/p/ai-190-scion-of-the-times
Source type: newsletter commentary, October 15, 2026. Read: accessible post text, 'Language Models Offer Mundane Utility' section.

Zvi quotes a social-media suggestion to send a photo of a credit card in chat and let an AI find a deal and order the item. He questions both the credential-sharing move and the shopper's ability to know whether the item or price is best without a check. The quote is a proposal, not evidence that anyone did this or suffered a loss.

Limit: opinion about a proposed workflow; no measured payment failure rate. Practical use: limit an agent to public product comparison; do not paste card photos into chat; use a separate approved checkout mechanism and explicit human confirmation of item, merchant, amount and payment before buying.
