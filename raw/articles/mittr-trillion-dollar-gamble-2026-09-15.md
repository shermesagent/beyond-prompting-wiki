# What must happen for AI's trillion-dollar gamble to pay off

Source: MIT Technology Review, David Rotman, 2026-09-15.

This analysis frames the hyperscaler data-center buildout as a large financial bet that depends on future AI revenues, broad productivity growth, continued pricing power, and enormous infrastructure spending. The agency issue is that the risk is not carried only by the companies building the systems. Utilities, communities, workers, ratepayers, pensions, and local power planning can become part of the risk surface.

For this wiki, the practical lesson is that AI control is not only a software question. When AI infrastructure enters budgets, facilities, contracts, and public utilities, the control surface has to include who pays, who owns, who can exit, and who absorbs failure.
