# Source note: Who Thinks First? Designing Productive Friction with Engage-to-Unlock GenAI
Authors: Xiaotian Su, Laura Rimell, Jiazheng Li, Amal Rannen-Triki, Ulrich Paquet, Lisa Anne Hendricks, Rida Qadri, Daphne Ippolito, Piotr Mirowski.
Source: arXiv:2610.01518v1, announced in cs.HC feed October 2, 2026; primary abstract checked at https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01518.
Scope: controlled writing experiment, N=398, four conditions: human-only, standard chatbot, engagement-gated unlock, and time-matched unlock.
Abstract-based finding: the engagement gate shifted time toward writing and away from evaluation without increasing total task duration. The gated group submitted more prompts than other AI-assisted conditions and had the highest accuracy per unit of evaluation time on evidence and inference errors. Not proof of longer-term learning or a district intervention.
Practice translation (editorial inference): jot a claim or outline before unlocking AI drafting; afterward check the most consequential factual claim against its source. Compare whether your initial idea survives.
