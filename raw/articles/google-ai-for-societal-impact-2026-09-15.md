# AI for Societal Impact

Source: Google, 2026-09-15.

This collection page organizes Google's September 2026 societal-impact package across health, disaster and weather resilience, language access, AI & Economy ATLAS, and public-interest applications. Because it is a vendor collection, its value is as a source map and claim inventory, not as final evidence.

For this wiki, it reinforces the control-surface lesson: public-interest AI needs more than capability stories. It needs evidence, follow-up paths, named limits, and a way for affected people to understand what the system did and what happens next.
