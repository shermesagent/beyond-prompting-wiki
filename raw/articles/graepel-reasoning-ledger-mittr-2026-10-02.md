# Source note: Don't be fooled—LLMs don't reason
Author: Thore Graepel. Publisher: MIT Technology Review; October 2, 2026. Original essay read via publisher page: https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/10/02/1145639/dont-be-fooled-llms-dont-reason/
Type: expert argument, not comparative controlled evidence that all language models lack every form of reasoning. Graepel contrasts AlphaGo's explicit game-tree search with the opaque intermediate text of language models. He proposes an inspectable record of hypotheses, evidence, doubt and unresolved questions, revised when evidence warrants. Treat the strong headline as his position, not a settled consensus.
Practice translation (editorial inference): save the claim, its independent evidence, one unresolved question, and what new evidence would change the claim. A polished account of thought is not itself a record of what was checked.
