# The Safety Culture at the Labs is Broken

Source: Zvi Mowshowitz, newsletter essay, published 2026-10-14 (per RSS feed); read 2026-10-18. This is a curator note of commentary, not a reproduction.

Mowshowitz argues that written rules and model reports cannot replace an institutional culture that can surface defects, bring in outside safety expertise and change course. He quotes former OpenAI employee David Robinson advocating safety-engineering practices from aviation and nuclear power. These are author and quoted-source positions, not independently established findings about every AI laboratory.

Practical takeaway: after a near miss, ask who can pause the process, who receives the report, and what changed before restart. Record the answer, not merely the existence of a safety policy.
