# Source note: Judgement and the Feeling of Agency When Instructing a Computer to Act

Johanna Didion, Diego Garaialde and David Coyle; arXiv:2610.03722v1, announced October 6, 2026. Primary abstract and metadata checked in the cs.HC RSS feed. Two experiments explored how people experience agency when instructing a computer. Participants experienced agency over its first action, but not a pre-reflective feeling of agency over the final outcome; for meaningful outcomes and specific commands, they later judged themselves to have caused the outcome. The authors recommend design that prompts deliberate reflection about responsibility rather than relying on a feeling of control. Practical translation: before approving a delegated result, ask what happened after the first action and which consequence you are choosing to own. The abstract does not establish a workplace intervention effect or district results.
