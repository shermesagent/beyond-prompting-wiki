# Source note: The Hackening

Publisher: Don't Worry About the Vase, Zvi Mowshowitz; published 2026-10-23 (page comments date), inspected 2026-10-25.

This is a long opinion/newsletter essay, not a measured deployment study. Zvi quotes an Internet Archive operator account saying staff briefly took the site offline, used Claude to develop fixes for older PHP versions, used local models to simulate attacks, deployed and strengthened protective controls. The operational distinction is that finding an issue and getting a tested fix to the affected service are separate jobs. The Internet Archive account is reported through Zvi's quoted post, not independently verified by this note. The essay also urges sustainable basic security practices rather than panic; its forecasts are not demonstrated outcomes.

Practical translation (ours): draw the path from alert to responsible person to official advisory to staged test to approved release and restore plan. On a public-data, non-production exercise only, time each handoff; do not infer that an AI-proposed patch is safe or that any particular organization should change production systems from this essay.
