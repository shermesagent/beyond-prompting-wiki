# Supporting Researcher Control in AI-Assisted Social Media Analysis

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22006
Authors: Qile Wang, Abhishek Karwankar, Shukria Muhammadi, Srikanth Kumar Perumalla, Abhin Suresh, Sunita Chandrasekaran, Kenneth E. Barner, Matthew Louis Mauriello
Publication date: 2026-11-18 (arXiv RSS announcement; version 1)
Evidence level: Publisher preprint abstract via arXiv RSS; full paper not appraised.

The AI4OSN platform gives researchers inspectable AI advice while they retain decisions about data collection, relevance labels and interpretation. In a user study with 18 participants working with X/Twitter data, reported usability averaged 79.7 on the System Usability Scale, with low reported cognitive workload. Novices showed descriptively greater agreement with AI than experienced participants; the abstract does not establish causation, generalize to school districts or measure better research conclusions.

Practical transfer: in an approved, public-material trial, keep the source, proposed label, reviewer change and final decision visible together. Ask a second reviewer to check one disputed label. Do not upload identifiable people, students or personnel data into an unapproved tool.
