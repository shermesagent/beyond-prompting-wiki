# Making AI-Assisted Claims Independently Challengeable: Publication Authority and a Protocol for Falsifiable Publication Records

- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17631
- Source type: Research paper
- Date: 2026-09-15
- Ingested: 2026-09-18
- Authors: Torsten Olivi Tiltack, Yifei Dong, Kun Yu, Xu Wang, Wei Liu, Jianlong Zhou, Ren Ping Liu, Fang Chen

## Abstract / Summary

AI-assisted claims can appear authoritative when evidence, analysis, human authorization, presentation, and correction history refer to different states. The authors develop Publication Authority as an exact-state, non-transferable, single-use publication capability and instantiate it in PAC-2026. Six obligations govern evidence, runs and artifacts, measurement disclosure, authorization, surface correspondence, and lifecycle continuity. Each yields a target-bound witness, localized counterexample, or localized unverifiability; none can compensate for another. Only a fresh, complete all-pass record derives the permit consumed by one atomic publication transition.

## Beyond Prompting Translation

Approving an AI-assisted output only means something if the approval refers to the exact version that ships, with the same evidence, artifacts, measurements, and surface the audience will see.

## Practice Hook

Use this source to strengthen practice routines that require visible evidence, source independence, state-specific approval, and saved reasoning — not just smoother AI output.
