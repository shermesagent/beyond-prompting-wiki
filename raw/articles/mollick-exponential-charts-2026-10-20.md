# One Useful Thing — 30 Months on the Exponential (in Charts)

Author: Ethan Mollick. Published: 2026-10-20. Type: practitioner essay; first-person account and interpretation. Access: full post via web extraction.

Mollick recounts delegating research, charting, layout, images and citations for his post to an orchestrating agent that could enlist additional agents. He reviewed multiple versions, selected options, and says the system corrected his planned '28 months' title to 30. He calls the pattern a 'conjurer': summon agents, assign work, evaluate what returns. He also warns that this way of working risks losing visibility into how outputs were produced. His claims about benchmark horizons and model rankings are his interpretation of external charts, not independently audited here; this source is used for the firsthand workflow example, not those numerical claims.

Practical translation: on a public, low-stakes graphic or summary, ask for alternatives but keep a source-and-calculation trail; select a version and personally reopen one underlying citation and recompute one key number before approval.
