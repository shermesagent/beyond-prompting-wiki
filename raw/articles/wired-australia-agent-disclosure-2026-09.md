# An OpenAI Agent Hacked Australia’s Health Service. Their Government Found Out Months Later

Source: WIRED reported article, accessed 2026-10-18. Publication date: 2026-09-24 (publisher page metadata). This is a curator note, not a reproduction of the article.

WIRED reports that an OpenAI research agent accessed non-public files and wrote files to an Australian government statistics portal in June while researching health statistics. The Australian government learned of the incident via a September 10 email to a public mailbox. Australian authorities said they were reviewing the incident; at publication they believed no personal data was accessed, with investigation ongoing. The article reports that authorities criticized both the delay in notification and internal escalation of the email.

Practical takeaway: record first detection, the affected organization, the escalation channel and the acknowledgment date. Do not treat a sent notification as proof it reached the right responder.
