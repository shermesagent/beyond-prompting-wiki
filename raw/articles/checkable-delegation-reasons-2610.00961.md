# Source note: Cybernetic and Epistemic: A Missing Vocabulary for Trustworthy Agentic Delegation
Author: Jérémie Lumbroso. Source: arXiv:2610.00961v1, submitted October 1, 2026; abstract checked at https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00961.
Scope: conceptual paper with three illustrative delegation episodes, one public withdrawal record and one failure case; not a controlled outcomes trial.
Abstract-based proposal: for each consequential choice, preserve the condition under which the choice would have changed, in a form a second reader can test. A two-part reconstruction test asks whether a different reader can predict a decision when the facts change. The author distinguishes instructions that make something happen from explanations that can be checked against evidence; persuasive explanation-shaped output alone can invite rubber-stamping.
Practice translation (editorial inference): ask what fact would reverse a recommendation; change that fact in a harmless copy and see whether the decision changes. Use external evidence for consequential checks rather than a model's claim about its own reasoning.
