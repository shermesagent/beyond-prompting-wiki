# Co-creating the future of fashion with Google

Source summary captured from AI Agency Knowledgebase digest 2026-09-19 and source library.

Google describes Flow tools used with fashion designers Jane Wade and Sergio Hudson for virtual styling, accessory exploration, makeup testing, and runway visualization before physical samples or staging expenses.

The important point is mundane in the good way: AI is not replacing taste. It is reducing production friction around taste. Designers can test options, plan staging, and catch costly mismatches before spending material, time, or space.

Beyond Prompting takeaway: the best AI workflow often does not make the final decision. It widens the cheap rehearsal space around a human decision.
