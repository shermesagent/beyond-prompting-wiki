# Comprehension Audits to Mitigate Risks from Automated AI Research

Authors: Ronald J. Bodkin, Bahrad A. Sokhansanj, Gillian K. Hadfield. Announced in arXiv cs.CY feed October 8, 2026; preprint. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10064

Primary RSS abstract proposes independent comprehension audits for AI research: responsible people explain AI-produced contributions and pause development if they cannot demonstrate understanding. An analysis of open-source AI projects reports more code output and less human review commentary per line, especially for automated accounts. This is a proposed assurance mechanism, not a trial showing audits improve outcomes; research-lab conditions do not automatically apply to schools.

Practical translation: before releasing a consequential AI-assisted draft, ask the accountable person to explain one key choice, point to supporting evidence, and name a condition that would stop release. This teach-back card is our exercise, not a validated instrument from the paper.
