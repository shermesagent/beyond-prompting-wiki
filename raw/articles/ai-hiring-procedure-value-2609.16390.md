# Do job seekers value procedure in AI hiring only for error correction? Evidence from a conjoint experiment

**Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16390

**Authors:** Chuyao Wang, Patrick Sturgis, Daniel de Kadt

**Date:** 2026-09-14

## Abstract / Extracted Summary

Job seekers valued appeal, opt-out, independent bias-audit rights, and human involvement even when the statistical error rate was not the only issue. Human involvement mattered about as much as a large error-rate reduction. Practical takeaway: people want the right to invoke a procedure because the procedure itself signals standing.

## Beyond Prompting Takeaway

For consequential AI, accuracy is not enough. If a person cannot appeal, opt out, or reach a meaningful human decision point, the workflow feels like power with no handle.
