# Scaling public input on AI policy with pluralistic agents

Authors: Jacy Reese Anthis, Ken Burke, David Madras, Crystal Qian, Meredith Ringel Morris, Logan Cross, Caroline Pantofaru, Utsav Prabhu. Published 2026-11-11. arXiv preprint 2611.16028.

> Most participants preferred participating themselves over either pluralistic agents or the baseline, but pluralistic agents recovered 68% of the baseline–direct gap in reported satisfaction and 82% in fairness.

Abstract excerpt only. Preregistered study with 746 human participants. Agent influence was sensitive to instructions from the represented persona; the paper recommends amplification of human input rather than replacement.
