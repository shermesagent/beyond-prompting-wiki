# The Social Society Helping Women Join the AI Boom

Publisher: WIRED interview by Zoë Schiffer; accessed 2026-10-24
URL: https://www.wired.com/story/aged-intelligence-society-interview/

## Directly read interview — attributed notes, not a verbatim archive
Shoshana Berger and Nina Willdorf describe invite-only small dinners where participants bring their own problem and laptop; they say they devote 15 minutes to guided tool experimentation. One founder reports using AI to critique her consulting proposal and changing the fee; another attendee reportedly uploaded a long care-insurance packet. These are personal reports, not controlled measures of outcomes or privacy authorization. Founders say they sometimes suggest a different use when an attendee does not want AI to write for her.
