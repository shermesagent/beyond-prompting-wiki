# Available but Unclaimed: An Empirical Study of Human-AI Synergy

**Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16793

**Authors:** Welsch, Rausch, Knierim, Kosch, Kuhn, Schmidt, Fernandes

**Date:** 2026-09-15

## Abstract / Extracted Summary

In a between-subjects study, 535 participants solved a 40-item reasoning battery unaided or with one of four frontier models. Assisted trials required consultation with the model, and each model also answered every item alone under matched elicitation. Human benefit increased when item-level model competence was higher, and deference also increased with competence. But post-advice confidence distinguished correct from incorrect answers less strongly than unaided confidence. About half the increase in model accuracy carried through to assisted human accuracy, and the carry-through differed by model. Practical takeaway: AI advantage is available but not automatically claimed. Workflows need selective-deference support that helps people take useful advice without surrendering independent reasoning.
