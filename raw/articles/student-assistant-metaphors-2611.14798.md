# Who (or what) are they prompting? How CS students conceptualize and use LLMs

Authors: Nikitha Donekal Chandrashekar, Margaret Ellis, Sehrish Basir Nizamani, Naren Ramakrishnan. Published 2026-11-10. arXiv preprint 2611.14798.

> Our results show that students most often conceptualized LLMs as human assistants, and context-rich prompting was the preferred style.

Abstract excerpt only. End-of-course survey of 283 consenting students in a post-CS1 course. The reported associations are exploratory, not evidence of effects in K–12 classrooms.
