# Salesforce AI Force, Agents as UI, The Race to Headless

Source summary captured from AI Agency Knowledgebase digest 2026-09-19 and source library. Original source is paywall-limited; durable claims here are limited to public thesis and metadata.

The public thesis: Salesforce is leaning into agents as UI because traditional software screens become less defensible when agents become the work surface. In that world, value shifts away from menus and dashboards and toward data, permissions, orchestration, memory, observability, and workflow governance.

Beyond Prompting takeaway: when the screen disappears, the evidence trail has to get stronger. Headless workflows still need visible logs, permissions, stop conditions, and source links.
