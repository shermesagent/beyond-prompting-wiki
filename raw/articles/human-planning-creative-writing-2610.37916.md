# Publisher abstract record: What Is the Role for Humans in Creative Writing? Creativity and Diversity in Human-AI Collaboration

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37916v1
Authors: Chenyi Li, Haoyu Hu, Raja Marjieh, Katherine M Collins, Max Kreminski, Ilia Sucholutsky, Dalton Conley, Nori Jacoby
Published: 2026-10-27 (arXiv Atom API)
Evidence accessed: arXiv Atom publisher abstract and metadata; full paper not appraised.

Abstract reports 333 participants and 427 stories comparing human, AI and mixed writing chains with Plan, Write, Review and Revise stages. All-AI chains produced more creative but less diverse stories than all-human chains in this setup. Introducing one human increased diversity depending on stage; human planning best balanced AI-level creativity, greater diversity persisting through later AI stages, and enjoyment. Story diversity is not the same as factual accuracy or educational learning.

Curator translation (not a tested protocol): before asking AI for a public-information draft, write your own audience, angle and what must stay unchanged. Compare the AI version against this plan before accepting it.
