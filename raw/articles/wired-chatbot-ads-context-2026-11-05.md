# Ads in Chatbots Have Arrived. Here’s What’s Working—and What’s Not

Source: https://www.wired.com/story/ads-in-chatbots-have-arrived-heres-whats-working-and-whats-not/
Publication date: 2026-11-05
Author: Paresh Dave
Evidence level: Directly read WIRED Model Behavior newsletter. Performance figures and data-handling claims are attributed to interviewed companies, not independent audit.

The article describes chatbot advertising that can be tailored using both a user's prompt and the AI's response. Koah's CEO says the company typically receives the conversation up to the ad call, de-identifies stored queries, and sees around 3% ad click-through; he declined further details on collection and storage. The article says Koah does not currently serve ads in apps where agents shop autonomously; suggestions about future agent-directed advertising are forecasts. Another chatbot company's CEO says the ad experience does not feel right yet for privacy and design. Practical translation: when a recommendation appears during a public-product comparison, distinguish source-supported criteria from sponsored placement, and ask what parts of a conversation an ad partner can receive. No private queries or district records in an unapproved service.
