# Source note: AI Is Making a Mess of Nurses' Schedules

Paresh Dave, WIRED, October 2, 2026. Directly read publisher article. Nurses interviewed by WIRED describe unwanted consecutive shifts, weak experience mix and a centralized appeal process for HCA's Timpani scheduler. HCA says nursing leaders make final decisions and reports operational gains; nurses dispute the quality of local correction. The reported patient safety concerns are allegations, not measured causal effects of the scheduler. Practical question: can a person close to the work identify a bad recommendation, reach someone empowered to change it in time, and verify the replacement schedule?
