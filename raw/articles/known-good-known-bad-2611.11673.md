# Known Good, Known Bad: A Ground-Truth Calibration Protocol for AI Judgment in Science

Publisher abstract read, not full paper. Preprint posted 2026-11-08. Author: Olga Lavinda.

An instructor-generated organic-chemistry demonstration used one verified reference and five deliberately altered variants, with six conversations and 18 responses from an unidentified backend on a free logged-out ChatGPT interface in July 2026. Neutral evaluation caught three of five intended deviations initially; authority-framed follow-up caught the two misses, and all five remained detected in a final line-by-line audit. In one scale case, the model found the unit problem but failed to repair the downstream calculation. The verified reference also drew speculative concerns. This is a small descriptive demonstration, not a validated comparison across models, classrooms or domains.

Curator application (not a tested prescription): Test an agent on a known-good public example and one safely altered example. Score both whether it flags the change and whether it fixes the final answer; do not treat a correct critique as a correct result.
