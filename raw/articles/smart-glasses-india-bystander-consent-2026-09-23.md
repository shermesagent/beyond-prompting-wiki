# Source notes — Smart glasses are already causing havoc in India

Anuj Behal, MIT Technology Review, 2026-09-23. Read from publisher on 2026-09-24. Reporting, not a controlled study.

The article reports Shubnam's account of being filmed at a Delhi protest without realizing a smart-glasses wearer was recording; a mocking video circulated online with abuse and lasting personal consequences. It also reports police use of smart glasses at student demonstrations and allegations in a legal petition; those allegations are not adjudicated findings. The device operator gets convenience while the recorded person may lack notice, consent or effective recourse. Meta says its active-capture LED cannot be disabled without disabling the camera; reporting describes modifications, so the technical dispute should not be represented as resolved. A reviewer should ask whose consent and remedy a new capture workflow requires before piloting it.
