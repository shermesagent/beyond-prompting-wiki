# Uneven Self-Reported AI Use: An Embedded Case Study Across Eleven Teams in a Software Organization

Authors: Daniele Verì
Published: 2026-11-10
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14154

## Abstract excerpt (source wording; not full paper)

We report a descriptive embedded single-case study of self-reported AI use in a software organization. The analysis retains 98 scored interview records: 65 collected with ten standardized team-level instruments and 33 with one distinct team-level instrument. On the standardized-form base, 36/65 respondents (55.4%) reported a numeric frequency score of 4 or 5; across the ten teams using standardized forms, scores range from 1/5 (20%) to 5/7 (71.4%). In a record-level cross-tab, 31/36 (86.1%) respondents scoring 4–5 and 28/29 (96.6%) scoring 0–3 reported no policy awareness or unclear policy details. The distinct-form group produced 31/33 (93.9%) at the same numeric cutpoint; the pooled 67/98 (68.4%) numeric summary is secondary because its verbal anchors differ. These counts describe the interviewed sample, rather than organizational prevalence, policy compliance, or the value of AI use.
