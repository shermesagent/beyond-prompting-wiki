# Closing the Analyze-to-Design Gap: An Authorization-Bounded Agent for Self-Driving Laboratories
Published: 2026-11-18. Authors: Yimeng Liu and colleagues. Source: arXiv preprint abstract, https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25043. Accessed 2026-11-20.

## Abstract capture
The authors describe an AI agent operating in a laboratory measurement workflow with human-defined campaign limits. State-changing operations are authorized, validated and auditable. The agent submitted measurement jobs, interpreted instrument data, flagged failed experiments and produced an oxidant ranking that matched scripted peak integration; chemists confirmed its spectral interpretations and failure assignments. The demonstration concerns one laboratory workflow, not evidence that other organizations should grant unsupervised AI actions.

## Curator note
Actionable transfer: separate permissions to inspect, draft and act; write down the exact action that needs human approval. The practice analogy is not a tested school or office deployment.
