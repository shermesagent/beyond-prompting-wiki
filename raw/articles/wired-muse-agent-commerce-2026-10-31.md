# WIRED — Meta’s Muse Could Upend All Kinds of Businesses. It Hasn’t Yet

Author: Paresh Dave. Published: 2026-10-31. Type: reported article. Access: full article via web extraction.

WIRED compares Meta's reported Muse activation and shopping claims with executives' accounts of limited agent-originated activity at a range of merchants. Meta said over 10 million people in the US and Canada activated Muse, and more than a quarter used it for shopping; separately, DoorDash described agent-originated orders as in the hundreds and Hilton called agent bookings insignificant. Those reports measure different things and do not directly disprove each other. Chipotle reported agent checkout difficulties with loyalty discounts and after-hours ordering. The article also notes Bank of America's warning that customers who share login credentials with AI assistants are responsible for activity from that access. Some retailers have not yet reported post-launch figures.

Practical translation: separate activated users, successful completed transactions, and authorized spending. Let an agent compare public offerings as a draft, but never hand over passwords or payment rights for a rehearsal. Confirm item, merchant, final price and payment method in an approved checkout before purchase.
