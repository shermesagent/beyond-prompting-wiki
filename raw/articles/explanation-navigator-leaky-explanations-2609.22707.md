# Explanation Navigator: Rectifying Out-of-Scope Human Interpretations of Leaky AI Explanations through Conversational Guidance

- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22707
- Date: 2026-09-19
- Authors: Yueqing Xuan, Kacper Sokol, Danula Hettiachchi
- Ingested: 2026-09-22

## Summary

AI explanations can leak: they simplify complexity, but correct interpretation still depends on hidden details. In an interview study, participants often filled in missing explanatory information and treated their guesses as understanding. Explanation Navigator detects mismatches between the user's question and the explanation's scope, then supplies missing context or redirects the user to a better explanation. In an online study with 316 participants, the approach helped people recognize and correct confabulated explanatory insights.

## Beyond Prompting Takeaway

This source supports today's theme: people do not need AI systems that simply do more for them. They need systems that keep the right human decision alive — what to decide, when to stop, what memory may travel, what an explanation does not show, and what agenda governs an agent before it acts.
