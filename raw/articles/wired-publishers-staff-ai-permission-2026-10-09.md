# Source note: Book Publishers Are Quietly Using More AI. Staff Are Revolting

Adam Morgan, WIRED, October 9, 2026. Reporting based on interviews with more than two dozen publishing workers, many anonymous, alongside company responses. Staff described AI-assisted publicity copy and pressure to propose uses; literary agents raised manuscript-input concerns, while a HarperCollins source said legal managers told editors not to paste full manuscripts into an open-loop tool. A Simon and Schuster spokesperson said only vetted tools are permitted and use is not mandatory; Hachette distinguished operational from creative uses. Allegations and policy statements are not a prevalence survey or legal finding. Practical translation: before delegating, separate permission to use a tool, permission to input someone's work, and permission to publish output. Read directly from WIRED on October 10.
