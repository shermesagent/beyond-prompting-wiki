# Source note: Connectedness, Cognitive Load, and Human-AI Oversight in Cyber Operations

Conklin, Gao and North; arXiv:2610.02384v1, submitted October 1, 2026 and announced in the October 5 cs.HC feed. Abstract and metadata read directly on arXiv. The authors propose a reference architecture for cyber review that considers both event risk and an operator’s capacity to review; maritime connectivity and workload signals are specific to that setting. The abstract does not report a tested workload reduction or validated school-office proxy. Practical translation: before routing a high-stakes item for approval, ask if the reviewer has time, access to the evidence, and a backup reviewer. Do not silently treat a busy reviewer as approval.
