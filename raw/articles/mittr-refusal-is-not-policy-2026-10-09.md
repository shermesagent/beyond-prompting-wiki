# Source note: We're putting too much faith in AI's ability to say no

Arthur Holland Michel, MIT Technology Review, October 9, 2026. Reported feature, not an experimental estimate of refusal rates. Refusal training and surrounding filters can fail or reject legitimate requests; who writes the refusal rule matters as much as whether the system refuses. Practical translation: distinguish a model's voluntary refusal from a workflow-enforced permission boundary and a human appeal route. Do not treat any particular model's refusal as a security guarantee. Read directly from the publisher on October 10.
