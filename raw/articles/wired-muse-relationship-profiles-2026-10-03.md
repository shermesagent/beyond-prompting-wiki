# Source note: Muse Creates Detailed Profiles of All Your Friends and Family

Lily Hay Newman, WIRED, October 3, 2026. Directly read publisher article. Extracted instructions shared with WIRED describe an hourly process that can build pages about the user's friends, family and coworkers from available context. Meta says each user's virtual machine is isolated, and users can wipe memories, disconnect services and inspect an audit log. Those are provider descriptions, not independent verification of deletion or consent from the people profiled. Practical question: whose information is inferred from a connector, and can an affected nonuser see or correct an inaccurate inference? Do not connect real contacts or student data for an exercise.
