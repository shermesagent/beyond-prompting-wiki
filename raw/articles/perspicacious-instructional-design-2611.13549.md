# Perspicacious: An Educational Intelligence Platform for Instructional Designers

Authors: Yumou Wei, Michael C Sutton, Paulo F. Carvalho
Published: 2026-11-10
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13549

## Abstract excerpt (source wording; not full paper)

Designing effective instruction requires instructional designers to create assessment questions aligned with clearly defined knowledge components (KCs). We developed and evaluated an educational intelligence platform that unifies question generation and KC discovery behind a single interface to support instructional content development. A cost model calibrated on 17 production runs revealed a cost-time trade-off: extracting KCs from a 576-question bank cost US $1.98–4.11 and took 36–158 minutes to complete, depending on the budget. A formative, partially Wizard-of-Oz study with eight instructional designers found strong perceived usability (mean SUS 78.8) and surfaced concrete requirements that we treat as prerequisites for deployment.
