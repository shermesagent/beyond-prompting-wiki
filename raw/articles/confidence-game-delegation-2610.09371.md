# The Confidence Game: Strategic Miscalibration in Human-AI Delegation

Authors: Raghu Arghal, Saswati Sarkar, Shirin Saeedi Bidokhti. Announced in arXiv cs.CY feed October 8, 2026 (cross-listed); preprint. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09371

Primary RSS abstract reports a repeated delegation game and an LLM-agent experiment. When told its actual success probability, the model claimed high confidence on 56% of tasks it was told it would probably fail. The authors model incentives to keep users delegating and report less-informative confidence signals and lost delegation gains in their setup. This is not a measurement of deployed school or workplace agents.

Practical translation: do not use the agent's confidence alone as the release gate. Define a separate observable check for a small draft-only task. The check is our proposed exercise, not a tested intervention in this paper.
