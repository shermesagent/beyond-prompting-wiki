# WIRED — An AI Agent Injected Drugs Into a Live Mouse’s Brain

Author: Emily Mullin. Published: 2026-10-31. Type: reported article. Access: full article via web extraction.

WIRED reports that biotech founder Gabriel Ocana Santero's team used an AI agent with a camera and robotic injection rig in a live mouse experiment. A veterinary surgeon performed the craniotomy; the agent selected a therapy from 19 options and controlled injection settings. The team required human approval of the injection plan, installed an emergency motor-disconnect button, and restricted access to equipment code. The first model refused to guide the needle; a second proceeded. A coordinate-system fault delayed the work; a separate agent repaired code without being told live surgery was under way. The team had not yet assessed whether the therapy reduced seizures. The account is reporting on a company's experiment, not independent proof of safety or treatment benefit.

Practical translation: for any high-impact agent workflow, name the human approval point, physical or software stop, forbidden changes, and who is informed when a repair happens during a live run. Use a public-document rehearsal only; this is not a recommendation for clinical or animal work.
