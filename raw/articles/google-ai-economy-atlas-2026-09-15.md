# New insights from Google's AI & Economy ATLAS

Source summary captured from AI Agency Knowledgebase digest 2026-09-19 and source library.

Google's AI & Economy ATLAS update reports that AI adoption is uneven by occupation and geography. The useful Beyond Prompting lesson is not merely that more people use AI. It is that usage patterns reveal where translation work is happening: India's creative sector over-indexes relative to the global average, while U.S. usage skews toward technical occupations.

The scientist-use survey is the practical signal: nearly half of surveyed scientists use AI daily and report saving almost seven hours per week, but the bottleneck moves downstream to validation, physical experimentation, and clinical/scientific follow-through. AI removes one constraint and exposes the next one.

Beyond Prompting takeaway: productivity claims are incomplete until you can name the new bottleneck. The orchestrator asks, "What did this make easier, and what got harder because of the new speed?"
