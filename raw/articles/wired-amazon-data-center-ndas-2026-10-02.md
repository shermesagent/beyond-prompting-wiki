# Source note: Amazon Says It Is Going to Stop Using NDAs for Data Centers

Molly Taft, WIRED, October 2, 2026. Directly read publisher article. Amazon says it no longer uses nondisclosure agreements with county officials on data-center projects. WIRED describes contractor-mediated agreements and says Amazon did not answer whether its new position covers contractors. The announcement is not proof that every project's tenant, water and energy assumptions or concessions are public. Practical question: before a decision, can affected people inspect the same information as the decision-maker, including work done through vendors? This is a civic-infrastructure analogy, not an AI workflow outcome study.
