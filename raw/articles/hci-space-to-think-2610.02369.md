# Source note: Automating the Application of HCI Principles: Skills for On-Demand UI Construction, the Human-AI Space to Think, and the Future of HCI

Conklin, Capra and North; arXiv:2610.02369v1, submitted October 1, 2026 and announced in the October 5 cs.HC feed. Abstract and metadata read directly on arXiv. This is a proposed research agenda, not a measured improvement trial. The authors propose a shared space to think in which the person clarifies a task and an AI generates an interface; human-computer interaction guidance would be stored as inspectable, editable skills. Practical translation: sketch the choices and checks a useful task form would show before asking AI to generate a finished answer. Check accessibility with an actual user before claiming it is accessible.
