# Who Is Doing the Thinking? Characterising Human Agency in First-Year Students' AI Interactions

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04783v1
Publication date: 2026-11-03
Authors: Ali Shakiba, Thomas Chaffey
Evidence level: Publisher preprint abstract only; full paper not appraised.

The authors propose a preliminary way to describe a student's contribution in the AI interaction rather than judging the finished AI output alone. They examine 962 prompts from structured AI journals by 105 first-year software engineering students across ten journalled weeks. Agency patterns varied more with the phase of work than with a fixed student trait; explicit constraints were rare. The study is a first-year university computing course, not K–12, and prompt analysis does not itself prove learning. Try a public-material rehearsal in which a participant records the task phase, the decision they made and one constraint before requesting a draft.
