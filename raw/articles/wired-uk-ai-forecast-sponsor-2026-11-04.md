# Source note: The forecast whose sponsor was hard to see

Ben Gartside, WIRED, published November 4, 2026; full article directly read November 5. WIRED reports that UK officials repeatedly used a £400 billion AI growth figure from a 2023 Google-commissioned Public First report, while a later government estimate gave a £55 billion–£140 billion range and a £120 billion central figure by 2030. A minister characterized the newer estimate as highly uncertain. PF Global says its research was independent and differences partly reflect slower-than-expected adoption. These are forecasts with differing assumptions, not observed economic gains or a causal estimate for one organization.

Practical translation (ours): when an AI business case contains a big projected number, record the sponsor, model year, adoption assumption and a local measure to check later. Do not treat a precise forecast as verified savings.
