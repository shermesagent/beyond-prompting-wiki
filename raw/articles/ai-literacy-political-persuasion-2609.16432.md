# A light-touch AI literacy intervention helps protect against AI political persuasion

**Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16432

**Authors:** Reed Orchinik, David Rand

**Date:** 2026-09-14

## Abstract / Extracted Summary

A brief warning that LLMs can be prompted to persuade and present information selectively reduced political belief change by about 48% in a 3,208-person study without broadly reducing trust in generative AI. Practical takeaway: disclosure works when it names the mechanism before exposure, not when it simply labels something as AI.

## Beyond Prompting Takeaway

Barriers to AI use are not solved by telling people “this is AI.” People need a tiny procedural handle before the interaction starts: what the system may be trying to do, how it may select evidence, and how to slow down before accepting the frame.
