# BTOS AI Core Question Updates

Source: https://www.census.gov/hfp/btos/downloads/AI%20Question%20Wording%20Updates.pdf
Publication date: 2025-12-03
Authors: U.S. Census Bureau
Evidence level: Directly read source; summary excerpt, not full reproduction

Census Bureau document says its business AI-use question was broadened from use in producing goods or services to use in any business function, following expert feedback and cognitive testing. A level shift led the Bureau to create a new time series beginning with data released December 4, 2025. The older and newer measures are not a clean adoption trend.
