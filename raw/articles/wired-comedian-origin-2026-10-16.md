# Source note: WIRED on copied comedians' routines

Primary source: https://www.wired.com/story/ai-generated-hot-girls-are-ripping-off-comedians-to-boost-their-horny-content/
Source type: reported article, October 16, 2026. Read: accessible article text.

WIRED identifies eight comedians whose videos were replicated by AI-presented accounts. The report includes creator allegations, example comparisons, statements about Meta's impersonation and AI-content policies, and a response from Meta's head of Instagram that the company wanted to address wholesale copying. Some accounts bore AI labels. A synthetic-content label and a non-impersonation policy do not, by themselves, settle the origin or permission of a copied routine.

Limit: reported cases and allegations, not a legal ruling or measured prevalence. Practical use: before publishing an AI-assisted output, keep the source, permission and human approval record; if origin cannot be established, hold release and route for review.
