# Source capture — Systematic review course

Title: Observing the Conduct of Systematic Reviews with Generative AI Support: An Experience Report from a Graduate Software Engineering Course
Authors: Danilo Monteiro Ribeiro, Gilberto Sussumu Hida
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26057
Announced: 2026-09-23 (arXiv cs.CY RSS); v1. This is an abstract-grounded summary, not a full-text review.

An observational single-day class activity with ten doctoral students in three groups piloted systematic reviews with and without generative AI. Researchers analyzed classroom observations, artifacts, and assistant conversations. AI reduced initial barriers, accelerated alternatives, and made methodological problems more explicit, but also encouraged excessive delegation, superficial validation, tool difficulties, and attention drifting from doing the review toward operating the tool. Authors propose human supervision, decision records, and critical reflection; the report is not a controlled effectiveness trial.

Practical takeaway: for any source-review workflow, record the human inclusion/exclusion decision and why before AI writes the synthesis. Check one rejected and one accepted source against the original documents, not just the generated summary.
