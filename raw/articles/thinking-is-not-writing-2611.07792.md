# Thinking Is Not Writing: Designing AI Chatbots to Connect Reasoning and Revision

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07792v1
Publication date: 2026-11-05
Authors: Lucile Favero, Tanja Käser, Juan Antonio Pérez-Ortiz, Nuria Oliver
Evidence level: Publisher Atom abstract read directly; full preprint methods not appraised.

The abstract describes Maike, a questioning chatbot designed to support argument diagnosis and movement between thinking and writing without generating essay text. In a classroom comparison, 65 secondary-school students used Maike or a control chatbot with the same interface, model and Think/Write modes. About 40% of students improved essay scores overall; Maike users engaged in more idea-level dialogue (56% versus 44%) and moved more iteratively between discussion and revision. Moving from discussed ideas to revisions was positively associated with score gains. The abstract does not say that 40% improved because of Maike, establish district-wide effects, or validate the exercise below. Practical translation for a public, low-stakes draft: ask for a question about your reason instead of a replacement paragraph; write a revision yourself and note whether the idea discussed actually changed the draft. Do not enter identifiable student writing in an unapproved tool.
