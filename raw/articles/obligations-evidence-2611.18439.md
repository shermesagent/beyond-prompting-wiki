# Between Obligations and Evidence: A Participatory Study of Translating the EU AI Act into Technical Requirements

Authors: Helma Torkamaan and 11 coauthors. Submitted 2026-11-13. arXiv preprint 2611.18439.

> The EU AI Act imposes technical and organizational requirements for high-risk AI systems, but applying these requirements involves contextual choices about what to measure and what evidence supports a compliance claim. We investigate these challenges through a participatory workshop involving 32 researchers, policymakers, and practitioners from different disciplines, using realistic scenarios in healthcare, recruitment, and public transport to examine how regulatory obligations are interpreted, translated into technical requirements, and evaluated in practice. Through a thematic analysis of workshop data, we identify recurring tensions between how regulatory obligations are translated into measurable system properties, how responsibility and human oversight are exercised in practice, and how the validity of compliance evidence is affected by changing operational conditions and system uses.

Publisher abstract excerpt, not full paper. Workshop findings are illustrative, not a trial of workplace or district outcomes.
