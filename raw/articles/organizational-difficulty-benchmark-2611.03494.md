# Harder Than the Task: From Technical to Organizational Difficulty in AI Benchmarking

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03494v1
Publication date: 2026-11-02
Authors: Victor Barres, Aden Barton, Nicolai J. Foss, Henrik M. Nielsen
Evidence level: Publisher preprint abstract only; full paper not appraised.

The authors argue that a difficult technical task is not the same as a difficult organizational task: real work involves interdependent people, distributed knowledge, differentiated authority, deadlines, conflicting interests and changes while work is underway. In their review, fewer than one quarter of prominent benchmarks claimed coverage of any organizational dimensions. The abstract does not demonstrate that a specific agent succeeds or fails in a school or business.
