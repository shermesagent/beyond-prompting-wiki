# Source note: School search with AI Overviews bundled

Paresh Dave, WIRED, published November 4, 2026; full article directly read November 5. Google says that starting next year schools can choose whether to enable Search, Gemini, and Notebook, but cannot separately disable AI Overviews or AI Mode within Search. The report quotes a school IT administrator, parent advocates, an education researcher and Google’s public response; it describes district workarounds, including a Fairfax County Public Schools browser extension, which may break if search changes. This is reporting on a platform decision, not an outcome study or a statement about Farmersville ISD settings.

Practical translation (ours): before promising a teacher a search-without-summaries choice, verify what the district administrator can actually control in the current configuration. For a public search demonstration, compare a generated overview with the linked originals; keep student accounts and records out of an unapproved test. A workaround is not the same as a supported policy control.
