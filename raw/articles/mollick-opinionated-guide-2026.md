# An opinionated guide to which AI to use to do stuff

Ethan Mollick. One Useful Thing (July 23, 2026)

**Key shift:** "Until recently, using AI meant talking to a model through a chatbot in a constant back-and-forth conversation. Now, it means using an agentic system, where the AI is capable of doing the equivalent of many hours of real human work in one go by combining the brains of an AI model with a set of tools that let it plan and act for you. Basically, an agentic system gives an AI a computer to use."

**Practical advice:**
- For low-stakes casual use: any free model is fine
- For high-stakes (medical, legal): use best models (Claude Opus/Fable, GPT-5.6 Sol at High thinking)
- For real work: ChatGPT or Claude at $20/month — they give AI access to a computer
- Two modes: virtual computer (provider-hosted, easier) vs your own computer (more powerful, more complex)
- Permission management is critical: leave approvals on for anything that sends, spends, or deletes
- Prompt injection risk: agents browsing the web can encounter malicious text
- Delegation reality: start a multi-hour job from your phone, check results later

**Naming confusion:** ChatGPT Work vs Claude Cowork; Sol vs Fable vs Opus; thinking levels. The ecosystem is powerful but poorly documented.
