# "ChatGPT, what am I missing?": Designing AI Workflows around Professional Task Structure to Shape Analytic AI Use

**Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16482

**Authors:** Ma, Jazmati, Chimenton, Mei, Lane, Gajos, Doshi-Velez

**Date:** 2026-09-15

## Abstract / Extracted Summary

General-purpose AI lets users choose what support to request, but leaves them to structure the support a professional task requires. The authors designed two scaffolded interfaces around a negotiation-analysis scaffold: one that presented a completed AI analysis and one that supported user-directed, incremental development. In an 800-participant randomized experiment, AI-supported conditions improved preparation coverage over unaided work, and scaffolded workflows improved coverage over chat. The user-directed workflow produced a broader range of analytic requests and lower subjective effort. Practical takeaway: professional AI should structure how the human and AI build the analysis together, not merely provide a blank chat box.

## Beyond Prompting Takeaway

This source supports the 2026-09-16 theme: real-world AI adoption works when task structure, assurance, and selective human judgment are built into the workflow instead of left to individual prompting habits.
