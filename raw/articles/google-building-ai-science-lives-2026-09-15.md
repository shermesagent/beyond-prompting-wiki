# Building AI to accelerate science and improve lives

Source: Google, James Manyika, 2026-09-15.

Google's societal-impact roundup includes science and health examples such as AlphaGenome Atlas, WeatherNext 3, Planetary Prediction Engine, AI-assisted breast-cancer screening finding 25% of interval cancers previously missed in a 175,000-woman NHS/Imperial study, TB X-ray screening across 40 locations in six countries, diabetic-retinopathy screening at 1.15M+ scans globally, and smartphone/wearable detection work.

The source is vendor-framed, so it should be treated as a map of claims to verify rather than neutral proof. The useful beyond-prompting lesson is that healthcare AI is strongest as detection infrastructure when paired with evidence thresholds, human follow-up, privacy protection, and treatment capacity. Detection without follow-up is automated anxiety.
