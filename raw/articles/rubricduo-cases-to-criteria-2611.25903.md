# From Cases to Criteria: Reusing Evaluation Knowledge for Task-Specific Rubric Generation
Published: 2026-11-19. Authors: Qiyan Deng, Long-Kai Huang, Kangfei Zhao. Source: arXiv preprint abstract, https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25903. Accessed 2026-11-20.

## Abstract capture
The RubricDuo method connects past question-and-rubric cases with general evaluation dimensions, shared and local concepts, and specific criteria. It drafts a rubric for a new question, then retrieves past criteria and their original contexts to adapt the checks. The authors report better alignment with human-authored rubrics and downstream AI judging than the automatic comparison methods tested on RubricBench and HealthBench. This does not mean an automatically drafted rubric is ready for a consequential decision without expert review.

## Curator note
Actionable transfer: save the checks that caught a real problem, but ask whether they fit the next task before reusing them. This is a practical extrapolation from a rubric-generation study, not a validated training exercise.
