# AI Companies Really, Really Want to Buy Your Work Files and Emails

Source: https://www.wired.com/story/ai-companies-really-really-want-to-buy-your-work-files-and-emails/
Publication date: 2026-11-06
Author: Steven Levy
Evidence level: Directly read WIRED Backchannel reported essay. Vendor spending forecasts and privacy assurances are attributed, not verified market totals or guarantees of anonymization.

Levy reports pitches to license workplace documents, emails, tickets and messages for AI training or testing. Vendors describe stripping identifying details; an Electronic Privacy Information Center lawyer warns that unstructured messages can still contain sensitive personal information. A proposed sale of Spirit Airlines digital assets, including internal communications, was on hold amid a flight attendants union objection. Anthropic told Levy it does not buy workflows from live companies like WIRED. A worker's contribution to a file does not grant every organization the right to license that person's correspondence or other people's information. Practical translation: in a fictional file inventory, separate permission to read for one job from permission to send outside, retain, license or train on the records. Escalate actual contract, consent and privacy questions to authorized reviewers.
