# Trust, but verify, audit and attribute: a human-in-the-loop framework for checking AI-produced research results

Authors: György Eigner, Patrik P. Süli, Roland Hollós
Published: 2026-11-09
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13042

## Abstract excerpt (source wording; not full paper)

A passing check certifies only the artefact that was checked. For proofs translated into the proof assistant Lean, it has been shown that this artefact may state something other than the author’s text, and that deciding whether a translation is faithful is undecidable in general. We propose a domain-independent, human-in-the-loop framework for checking AI-produced results. Validated, agent-usable tools with different grounds of trust check the results; a fidelity audit asks whether each check addressed the claim as stated; the human decides at fixed points; and a dialog report attributes the work to the human and to the AI. We describe two automatic implementations of the tool layer, for mathematics and statistics, and run the mathematical one on a two-claim example. An ambiguous claim was split into two readings, one refuted and one proved.
