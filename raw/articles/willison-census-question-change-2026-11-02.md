# The U.S. Census Bureau and “Super Intelligence”

Source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/2/census-bureau-super-intelligence/
Publication date: 2026-11-02
Authors: Simon Willison
Evidence level: Directly read source; summary excerpt, not full reproduction

Willison recounts rebuilding a public Census business AI-use chart and checks the underlying question language. He shows why the November 2025 shift from producing goods or services to any business function makes a continuous trend misleading; he also notes that the October 2026 Super Intelligence wording had not yet affected published results at the time he wrote. This is a firsthand walkthrough, not an independent causal estimate. Try adding a question-wording note to a public chart.
