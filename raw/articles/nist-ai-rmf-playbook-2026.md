# Source note: NIST AI Risk Management Framework Playbook

National Institute of Standards and Technology, AI RMF Playbook page updated 2026-06-10; underlying framework released 2023-01-26. Official voluntary guidance, not an experiment or mandatory K-12 rule. Its four functions are Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage. NIST says users may tailor suggestions to their context. Practical translation: before a team calls an AI workflow safe, identify the owner, describe the affected task and people, choose a measurable check, and write the response when the check fails. No evidence here that any particular school district achieved a measured benefit. This is an older source newly added for today's glossary rotation.
