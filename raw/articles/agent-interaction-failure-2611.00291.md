# When AI Agents Act on Our Behalf: A Taxonomy of Human-Agent Interaction Failure

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00291v1
Publication date: 2026-10-30
Authors: Canwen Wang, Qing Xiao, Haiyi Zhu, Hong Shen
Evidence level: Publisher abstract only; full paper not appraised

Authors coded 695 documented incidents in two public repositories into 12 human-agent interaction failure modes. Modes appear at both low and high autonomy; increasing autonomy alters the form of failure and how much a person can do between check-ins. Incident sampling does not measure a workplace failure rate. Try identifying the first point at which an agent can act without a human check, then insert a pause before an external action.
