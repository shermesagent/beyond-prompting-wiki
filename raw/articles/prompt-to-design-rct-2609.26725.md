# Source capture — Prompt-to-design RCT

Title: Does AI Save Time on Product Design? A Randomized Controlled Experiment of AI Prompt-to-Design Workflows
Authors: Remy Stewart, Olabode Anise, Andrew Hogan, Augustus Griffin
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26725
Announced: 2026-09-23 (arXiv cs.HC RSS); v1. This is an abstract-grounded summary, not a full-text review.

A randomized controlled trial recruited 50 product designers and 50 product managers for three standardized design tasks with or without Figma Make. Among participants who completed the tasks, access to the tool was associated with approximately 20 percent shorter completion times, with larger gains among product managers. The reported speed result is conditional on task completion, and the abstract does not establish improved design quality, deployment readiness, or a universal benefit for professional designers.

Practical takeaway: time a small, repeatable drafting task and record completion, quality against a rubric, and rework, not just speed. Give managers a low-stakes mockup task while designers keep review authority for the actual product decision.
