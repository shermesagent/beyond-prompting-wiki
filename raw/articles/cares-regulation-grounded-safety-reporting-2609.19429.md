# CARES: A Conversational AI System for Regulation-Grounded Safety Reporting in Construction Education

- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19429
- Source type: Research paper
- Date: 2026-09-16
- Ingested: 2026-09-18
- Authors: Fan Yang, Jiabin Wu, Yuan Tian, Jiansong Zhang

## Abstract / Summary

Construction safety reporting often relies on manual logs and static templates that provide limited feedback and leave daily activities disconnected from relevant regulations. CARES integrates regulatory guidance into daily reporting through proactive multi-agent dialogue, retrieval-augmented generation, and automated report generation. Regulatory sources and the evolving report are displayed alongside the dialogue to support user review and correction. A preliminary evaluation with 15 construction management students found faithfulness of 0.74 and answer relevance of 1.00, while initial retrieval ranking remained an area for improvement.

## Beyond Prompting Translation

A useful AI reporting workflow keeps the source evidence and the draft side by side, so the human can correct the report while the context is still visible.
