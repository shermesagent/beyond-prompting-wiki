# Publisher abstract record: When Does GenAI Use Support Critical Thinking? A Cross-Cultural Structural Account of Transactive Memory with GenAI

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38910v1
Authors: Mahir Akgun, Sacip Toker
Published: 2026-10-28 (arXiv Atom API)
Evidence accessed: arXiv Atom publisher abstract and metadata; full paper not appraised.

Abstract describes a structural analysis of 891 college students in the United States and Türkiye doing AI-assisted academic work. Self-appraised thinking ability and confidence in the partnership did not directly predict critical thinking in the reported model. Experience mattered through coordinating who did which thinking and actively reworking AI output rather than accepting it unchanged. This is a modeled association, not a causal training trial or evidence of a K–12 district effect.
