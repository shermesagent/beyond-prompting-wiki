# Source note: Rethinking Inline Citation Verification in Scholarly Communication

Xinrui Fang, Reese Fairchild, Nasi Wang, Anran Xu, Simo Hosio, Sylvain Malacria and Koji Yatani; arXiv:2610.05355v1, announced October 6, 2026. Primary abstract and metadata checked in the cs.HC RSS feed. Exploratory interviews with 12 reviewers and a survey of 203 reviewers from human-computer interaction and AI venues found that perceived citation importance, verification practices and desired AI autonomy varied by citation type, reviewer and research background. The authors call for support suited to the claim and for shared responsibility among authors, reviewers and the broader community. Practical translation: for one important claim, open its cited source, verify that the source actually supports the sentence, and record who checked it. This paper describes reviewer practices; it does not demonstrate that an AI citation checker is accurate or that its approach transfers directly to schools.
