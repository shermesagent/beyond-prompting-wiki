# This Smart Ring Wants to Be a Remote Control for Your AI Agent

Source: https://www.wired.com/story/aina-smart-ring/
Publication date: 2026-11-05
Author: Julian Chokkattu
Evidence level: Directly read WIRED product reporting. Product capabilities are reported plans and company claims, not independently tested adoption or safety outcomes.

The Aina Ring is announced as a small display and joystick for approving or rejecting suggested agent actions without opening a phone. The founder describes a future of frequent yes/no decisions for actions such as ordering a ride or sending a lateness email. The report says suggestions may draw on location, calendar, email and connected services; users may create their own cues. The founder says the display should confirm whether an action went through. WIRED notes earlier AI gadgets have failed to stick and that supported actions may depend on app integrations. The source does not test whether quick approvals improve decision quality or preserve consent. Practical translation: rehearse a request using a public, low-stakes draft; write the exact action, data access, evidence, and what confirmation would show completion before pressing yes. Do not connect real accounts or purchase the product for the exercise.
