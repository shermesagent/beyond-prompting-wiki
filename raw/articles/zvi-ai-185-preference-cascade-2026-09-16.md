# AI #185: Preference Cascade

**Source URL:** https://thezvi.substack.com/p/ai-185-preference-cascade

**Authors:** Zvi Mowshowitz

**Date:** 2026-09-16

## Abstract / Extracted Summary

Zvi tracks a public conversation shifting toward monitorability, cyber incidents, governance, and whether institutions can show visible procedures before the next incident rather than after it. Practical takeaway: trust increasingly depends on whether labs and governments can produce recourse, receipts, and disclosure timelines.

## Beyond Prompting Takeaway

The useful lesson is not “panic.” It is “show the procedure before people need it.” If the first visible process appears only after harm, people will read it as cleanup, not governance.
