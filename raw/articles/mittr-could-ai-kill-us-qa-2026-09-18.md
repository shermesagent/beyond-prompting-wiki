# Could AI really kill us all? Your questions, answered

Source: MIT Technology Review, Will Douglas Heaven and Grace Huckins, 2026-09-18.

This Q&A separates different kinds of AI risk: current harms, frontier-agent control failures, biosecurity concerns, self-fulfilling discourse, and unresolved alignment uncertainty. The practical value is the both-and frame: take risk seriously without turning every conversation into apocalypse theater.

For people moving beyond prompting, the source supports one habit: translate risk claims into controls. What should be logged? What needs human approval? What should stop the system? What evidence would make the workflow safe enough for its current autonomy level?
