# I Used NYT Cooking’s New AI-Powered Recipe Modifier, and It’s Actually Pretty Good

Joe Ray, WIRED, November 8, 2026. Directly read reported hands-on review; author/date checked against WIRED RSS.

The New York Times Cooking iOS beta modifies existing recipes for ingredients, equipment and dietary preferences. Changes are highlighted; readers can toggle back to the tested original, and modified versions are labeled as AI-made and not tested or endorsed by the original recipe creators. Editors helped write the rules and evaluated behavior. Ray tested multiple adaptations, found a chicken-breast substitution that left cooked meat simmering too long, and withheld most of the meat from the pot. The product redirects some requests that depart too far from the original recipe; NYT said users will be able to hide the feature. Reader objections in the comment thread are reported, not a representative survey.

Transferable lesson (curator inference): when AI changes a trusted source, show the exact diff, preserve the original, label the altered output as untested and let people decline the feature. This is one reviewer's experience and vendor statements, not a controlled safety test or an endorsement for dietary or allergy decisions.
