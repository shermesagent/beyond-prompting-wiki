# Source note: Macmillan staff on the NOA workplace chatbot

Adam Morgan, WIRED, published October 28, 2026. Direct article read October 29.

Employees interviewed by WIRED describe a celebratory 2024 launch of the NOA chatbot while some human assistants found its name offensive, found it unreliable, and questioned advice to put portions of manuscripts into it. Staff were also encouraged to use it to draft performance goals. A company email announced its sunset in October 2025. Macmillan's spokesperson disputes the account of the rollout without specifying the inaccuracies, and says Gemini is used for business but leadership did not direct its use to evaluate agent submissions. These are contested employee accounts and company responses, not an adoption experiment or measured failure rate.

Practical translation (ours): before a team pilot, ask workers which task is worth trying, what material is approved for input, what would count as useful output, and how to stop. A launch party does not establish fit or trust. Do not upload unpublished manuscripts, student work, or personnel material to an unapproved tool.
