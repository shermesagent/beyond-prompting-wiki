# I watched a humanoid robot clean my apartment. It felt like a taste of the future.

Source: https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/06/1148090/i-watched-a-humanoid-robot-clean-my-apartment-it-felt-like-a-taste-of-the-future/
Publication date: 2026-11-06
Author: Grace Huckins
Evidence level: First-person reported trial, not independent capability benchmark or evidence of widespread autonomous cleaning.

Huckins booked two visits from Tau Robotics. Human operators piloted robots remotely using cameras; some actions involved a human performing motions that the robot copied. A controller's viewing equipment overheated during the first visit, causing an early stop; the second visit cleaned more successfully but still had slips and required human support. The company's claim that certain tasks can run unaided is attributed, not independently established across settings. Head and wrist cameras also raise a practical access question inside a private home. Practical translation: before calling an AI service autonomous, ask who watches, who intervenes, what data they see, and what happens if their connection or equipment fails.
