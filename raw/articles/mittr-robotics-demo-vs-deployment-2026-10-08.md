# Source note: AI breakthroughs in robotics won't change your life any time soon

Jamie Condliffe, MIT Technology Review, October 8, 2026. Reported feature with researchers' competing views, read directly on October 11. Lab demonstrations show real advances at narrow tasks, while new surroundings and multi-step jobs still create failures. The article notes that polished public robot demos can hide scripted motion or remote human control; one reported attempt at putting a sweet potato in an air fryer made progress without completing the task. Commercial trials cited involve restricted jobs in controlled settings, not general household independence. This is reporting on robotics, not a study of office agents. Practical analogy only: ask who or what did the hard part of a demo, repeat the task under changed conditions, and name the fallback when it cannot finish.
