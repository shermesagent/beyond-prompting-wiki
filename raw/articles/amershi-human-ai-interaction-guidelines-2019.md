# Source note: Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction

Saleema Amershi and colleagues, Microsoft Research, CHI 2019. The paper proposes 18 human-AI interaction design guidelines, evaluated over several rounds including a study with 49 design practitioners applying them to 20 AI-enabled products. The publication page says the guidelines are relevant across scenarios and identifies remaining knowledge gaps. The page does not show a trial of modern long-running agents or a K-12 outcome measure. Practical translation: test the handoff as an interaction: can a person discover what the system did, correct it, and recover when it is wrong? This is an older foundational source newly integrated, not a fresh 2026 study.
