# Apps for Caregivers Get a Dose of AI, but That Might Not Be Enough

Source: https://www.wired.com/story/apps-for-caregivers-get-a-dose-of-ai-but-that-might-not-be-enough/
Publication date: 2026-11-07
Author: Jaclyn Greenberg
Evidence level: Directly read WIRED reported article and first-person caregiver account; product features are descriptions, not independent clinical validation.

Caregiving apps can keep schedules, medication reminders and care notes together. The reported Citizen Health Ari beta can pull medical records, patient portals and calendar events for appointment preparation, but the article does not establish clinical effectiveness. Bioethicist I. Glenn Cohen notes that many consumer apps have not been reviewed as medical devices, and data entered by a person into a consumer app may not have the protections users assume. Trainer Denise M. Brown says keeping the record current often lands back on already overloaded caregivers when professionals do not update it. A shared handoff helps only when the people who must maintain it can actually do so. Practical translation: on paper with fictional details, name the update owner, consent and access boundary, and who checks a stale note before relying on a summary. No health or student records in an unapproved service.
