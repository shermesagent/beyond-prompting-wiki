# The Dot and the Swarm

Author: Ethan Mollick. One Useful Thing, 2026-10-01.
URL: https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/the-dot-and-the-swarm

## Attributed source notes (article read directly)
Mollick revises his earlier view that humans would need to carefully organize teams of AI agents. In his personal examples, agents arranged their own subtasks from a short brief; he argues that human direction may increasingly be about goals and reassessment rather than detailed org charts. He explicitly says he does not know how well such systems handle long routine organizational work and warns that self-organization can head in unwanted directions. His cited large-scale mathematics story and model-release claims are reported by him, not independently verified here; they are not evidence of a general deployment outcome.

## Curator interpretation
Before choosing an agent's organization, name the human goal, permissions, checkpoint and stop rule. Self-organizing work makes the checkpoint more important, not less. His examples are practitioner anecdotes, not controlled studies. Try on a public, low-risk task with no account permissions.
