# Source note: Words or Deeds? How LLMs Predict Users' Decisions in Human-Agent Dialogue

Baiqiao Zhang and colleagues, arXiv:2610.36813v1, submitted October 27, 2026. Publisher Atom abstract read October 29; full paper not appraised.

In a repeated game with 42 users and five personality-framed agents (1,260 rounds), the authors compared eight open-weight models' predictions from prior decisions and dialogue. Prior choices predicted behavior; adding dialogue brought no detectable overall gain, and predictions followed statements too readily when a player later broke a stated choice. This is a game prediction study, not an employee adoption survey or evidence that workers lie about AI use.

Practical translation (ours): ask for one optional, approved-task trial and inspect whether it was useful instead of inferring uptake from stated enthusiasm. Do not use an employee's statements or usage logs as a character judgment; ask for voluntary task feedback and respect non-use.
