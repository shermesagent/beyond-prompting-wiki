# Anticipatory Human Oversight of Agentic AI: A Philosophical Account

- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24242
- Date: 2026-09-21
- Authors: Kevin Baum, Maximilian Kiener, Markus Langer, Johann Laux
- Ingested: 2026-09-22

## Summary

Reactive human oversight reaches structural limits with agentic AI. If humans intervene in every action, autonomy disappears; if they only review aggregate patterns, cumulative harms may become visible too late. The authors argue for anticipatory oversight: specifying the normative agenda before the agent acts, then refining it through specification, runtime, and inspection. Escalation conditions connect anticipatory and reactive oversight, creating responsibility before action rather than only blame afterward.

## Beyond Prompting Takeaway

This source supports today's theme: people do not need AI systems that simply do more for them. They need systems that keep the right human decision alive — what to decide, when to stop, what memory may travel, what an explanation does not show, and what agenda governs an agent before it acts.
