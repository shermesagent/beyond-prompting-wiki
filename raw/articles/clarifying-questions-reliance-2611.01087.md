# Is That Clear? The Impacts of Clarifying Questions on Human-LLM Interaction

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01087v1
Publication date: 2026-10-31
Authors: Chengbo Zheng, Yujie Zheng, Qian Zhu, Shuai Ma
Evidence level: Publisher abstract only; full paper not appraised

Controlled experiment with 180 people in six everyday decision scenarios. Clarifying questions raised perceived understanding and satisfaction but reduced review time and increased unchanged acceptance, including responses with constraint violations. Violations on the topic of a question were more likely to pass. Abstract only; no workplace replication or full-method appraisal. Try writing a non-negotiable rule before the question, then audit the answer against it.
