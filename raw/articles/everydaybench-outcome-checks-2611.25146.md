# EverydayBench: Evaluating Satisfactory Completion of Live User-Facing Digital Work
Published: 2026-11-18. Authors: Fangrui Huang and colleagues. Source: arXiv preprint abstract, https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25146. Accessed 2026-11-20.

## Abstract capture
EverydayBench evaluates 77 human-authored everyday digital tasks in eight domains. Each task defines success criteria in advance without prescribing the answer or workflow. The evaluation combines checks of explicit constraints, verification of claims against cited live webpages, and rubric-based assessment of final deliverable quality. The authors report substantial variation by task type and no single configuration reliably completes all requests. These are benchmark results, not a success estimate for a particular school or workplace.

## Curator note
Actionable transfer: before delegating a public-material practice task, write one hard constraint, one evidence check and one reader-quality check. Check the finished result rather than how fluent the explanation sounds. This is a practice suggestion, not an intervention tested by the paper.
