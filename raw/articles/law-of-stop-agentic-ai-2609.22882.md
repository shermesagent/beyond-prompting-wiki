# The Law of Stop: Interruptibility, Injunctions, and the Governance of Agentic AI

- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22882
- Date: 2026-09-19
- Authors: Oren Perez
- Ingested: 2026-09-22

## Summary

Interruptibility for agentic AI is not a red button. It is an institutional practice made of technical affordances, interruption authority, epistemic triggers, and epistemic standing. Perez analyzes recent stop cases and governance instruments, arguing that distributed agentic systems expose gaps: a stop in one place may not halt activity elsewhere, and the missing stop is often legal or procedural rather than technical. A layered law of stop requires emergency authority, evaluator access to evidence, and safeguards when stopping fails.

## Beyond Prompting Takeaway

This source supports today's theme: people do not need AI systems that simply do more for them. They need systems that keep the right human decision alive — what to decide, when to stop, what memory may travel, what an explanation does not show, and what agenda governs an agent before it acts.
