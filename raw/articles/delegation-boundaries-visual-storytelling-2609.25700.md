# Source capture — Delegation boundaries in visual storytelling

Title: Harnessing LLMs Without Surrendering Control: Delegation Boundaries in Visual Data Storytelling Authoring
Authors: Zhuojun Jiang, Yuki Ueno, Chris Bryan
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25700
Announced: 2026-09-23 (arXiv cs.HC RSS); v1. This is an abstract-grounded summary, not a full-text review.

Interviews with 12 expert visual data storytellers found they rarely treated language models as autonomous storytellers. They delegated execution-oriented tasks while retaining control over narrative intent and meaning. The authors report AI assistance was most productive after human seeding and constraint-setting, and labor shifted from production to verification. This is a small qualitative study, not a measured population-wide productivity gain.

Practical takeaway: before an agent drafts a chart or report, write the intended claim, audience, and what must remain undecided by the tool. Ask for a draft with data provenance; review both the figures and the story the figures appear to tell.
