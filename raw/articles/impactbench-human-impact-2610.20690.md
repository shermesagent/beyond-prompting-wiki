# Source note: ImpactBench

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20690
Source type: arXiv research preprint, submitted October 14, 2026. Read: publisher abstract and author metadata, not full text.

The authors describe an expert-guided benchmark of model behavior affecting psychological, physical and social wellbeing. It has 809 metrics from 26 benchmarks and evaluates ten models in 48,540 *simulated* teen and adult conversations. They report desirable-behavior promotion in 68.7% of evaluations and harm avoidance in 53.1%. In reviewed cases, majority expert ratings agreed with the automated judge in 83.3% (kappa 0.667). Scores link back to metrics and conversation transcripts.

Limit: simulations and an audited judge do not establish effects on actual people or validate a particular school deployment. Practical use: before calling a score 'safe', open one metric and transcript, define the human outcome to test, and keep a separate human review path.
