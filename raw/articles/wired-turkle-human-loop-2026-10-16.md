# Maybe Chatbots Shouldn’t Get to Say ‘I’ or ‘Me’

Source: Steven Levy's WIRED interview with Sherry Turkle, published 2026-10-16; article read 2026-10-18. This is a curator note, not a reproduction of the interview.

Turkle argues that in some workplace arrangements the people called 'human in the loop' are assigned cleanup work without the staff or power to change the system. She also argues against first-person chatbot language in intimate interactions. These are interviewee arguments, not an experimental finding that banning pronouns improves outcomes.

Practical takeaway: before calling a workflow human-supervised, verify that the designated reviewer has time to review, authority to stop and a way to change the workflow after a failure.
