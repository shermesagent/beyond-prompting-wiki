# Reducing Catastrophic Risk from AI with Systematic Monitoring and Evaluation of Rogue AI Progression

**Source:** arXiv:2609.03189 (Bauer, Kegelmeyer, Begoli, Sadovnik, Emerson, Corley, Generous, Moore, Bartoldson, Goldhan, Goldman, Greaves, Vermeer, MacLennan, Schulker, VanHoudnos, Bansemer & Bengio), published 2026-09-03

**Summary:** A national-security-flavored inter-lab group (Sandia, ORNL, RAND-adjacent, universities) proposes replacing vibes-based worry about AI risk with **systematic monitoring**: a structured framework of behavioral indicators that may signal progression toward catastrophic capability or behavior. The approach is deliberately borrowed from cybersecurity and national security practice — fields that long ago learned you cannot respond to threats you did not define in advance.

The framework's core move: establish clear **metrics, indicators, and thresholds** across multiple dimensions of AI capability and behavior *before* anything happens, then run evidence-based monitoring protocols against those pre-committed definitions. Instead of asking "is this dangerous?" in the moment, you ask "has it crossed the threshold we named for this indicator?"

**Bottom line:** monitoring is only as good as the thresholds you commit to before the incident. The same discipline applies at any scale of delegation: name what would count as failure, define the indicator that would reveal it, and set the threshold that triggers action — while you are calm, not while the fire is burning.
