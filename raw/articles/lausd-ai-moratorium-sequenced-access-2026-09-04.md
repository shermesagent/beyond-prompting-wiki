# GovTech / LA Daily News — LAUSD Temporarily Bans Generative AI on School-Issued Devices

Source URL: https://www.govtech.com/education/k-12/lausd-temporarily-bans-generative-ai-on-school-issued-devices
Publication: Los Angeles Daily News via GovTech
Author: Teresa Liu
Date: 2026-09-04

## Summary

Los Angeles Unified School District temporarily blocked student access to standalone generative AI and embedded Google Workspace AI features on district-issued devices while it develops policy. Teachers retained access with professional development.

The decision highlights a real tension: moratoria can protect students while policy catches up, but they can also widen the shadow system. Students with personal devices continue experimenting outside supervision, while students dependent on school-issued devices lose guided practice.

## Beyond Prompting Takeaway

AI access should not be binary. A better pattern is sequenced access: start with concepts and risks, move to supervised teacher-led use, then gradually allow independent practice with reflection, disclosure, and rollback conditions. The practical question is: what must be true before the next degree of autonomy is allowed?
